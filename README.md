# Es Bangla - Official Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Official, production-ready Privacy Policy and Terms of Service website for the **Es Bangla** mobile application (Class 12 Higher Secondary - Semester 3 & 4 Learning Platform).

Complies fully with Google Play Developer Console requirements, Google Play Families Policy, and COPPA/GDPR student privacy guidelines.

- **Privacy Policy**: [`index.html`](index.html)
- **Terms of Service**: [`terms.html`](terms.html)
- **Styling**: Modern, responsive [`style.css`](style.css)

## 🚀 How to Host This Website for Free (Instant Setup):

### Option 1: GitHub Pages (Recommended - 1 Click)
1. Open this repository on GitHub: [https://github.com/airstudent2/es-bangla-privacy-policy](https://github.com/airstudent2/es-bangla-privacy-policy)
2. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (on the left sidebar).
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. In ~60 seconds, your live public URL will be ready at:
   👉 **`https://airstudent2.github.io/es-bangla-privacy-policy/`**

---

### Option 2: Netlify (Free & Instant)
1. Go to [Netlify.com](https://app.netlify.com/) and click **"Add new site"** → **"Import an existing project"**.
2. Connect to GitHub and select `es-bangla-privacy-policy`.
3. Click **Deploy**. Your custom Netlify link (e.g. `https://es-bangla-privacy.netlify.app`) will be generated.

---

### Option 3: Vercel (Free & Instant)
1. Go to [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com/) and click **"Add New Project"**.
2. Import `es-bangla-privacy-policy`.
3. Click **Deploy**. Your live Vercel URL (e.g. `https://es-bangla-privacy.vercel.app`) will be active immediately.

---

## 📱 App Integration:
Once deployed, copy your live URL and connect it to the **Privacy Policy** action tile inside the Es Bangla app profile screen.
