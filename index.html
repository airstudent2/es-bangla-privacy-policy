Privacy Policy for Es Bangla

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, safety, and security of Higher Secondary (Class 12) students using the Es Bangla learning platform.

📅 Last Updated: October 30, 2026 • ✅ Effective Date: October 30, 2026
1. Overview 2. Information We Collect 3. Use of Information 4. Third-Party Services 5. Security Standards 6. Student & Youth Privacy 7. Data Deletion Rights 8. Contact Us

📖 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Es Bangla ("we", "our", or "us"). Es Bangla is a dedicated educational mobile application designed specifically for West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education (WBCHSE) Class 12 students preparing for Semester 3 and Semester 4 examinations.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, protects, and discloses your information when you browse our home syllabus, take mock tests, watch video lectures, and read study notes.

Our Student-First Promise: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. All collected data is strictly used to deliver educational content, track mock test analytics, and authenticate subscription access.

📱 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Es Bangla (as a Guest or a Registered User), we collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Registration & Login: When you register or sign in with Google or Email/Password, we collect your display name, email address, and authentication user ID (UID).
  • Academic Preferences: Your selected Semester (Semester 3 or Semester 4) and preferred subjects.
  • Subscription & Access Keys: Activation keys and entitlement codes redeemed to unlock premium video courses or mock test series.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Mock Test & Quiz Analytics: Questions answered, time taken per test, accuracy score, strength/weakness topics, and test history for generating personalized performance charts.
  • Study Reminders: If you schedule daily study reminders, alarm timing preferences are stored securely on your local device.
  • Device & Usage Data: Android OS version, device model, app version, and anonymous crash/error logs to maintain app stability.
Guest Exploration Mode: You can explore our home screen, syllabus guides, and take free mock tests without creating an account. In Guest mode, your device generates a temporary local session and no personal email is collected.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for educational, operational, and customer support purposes:

  • To provide Class 12 video lectures, chapter notes, and mock tests for Semester 3 and 4.
  • To maintain and synchronize your test scores and performance analytics across app sessions.
  • To verify subscription status and restore previously redeemed entitlements even if you clear app cache or switch devices.
  • To send scheduled study reminders and important examination announcements (notifications can be disabled anytime in device settings).
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent access, unauthorized key redemption, or unauthorized content distribution.

☁️ 4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To provide secure cloud storage, authentication, and notifications, Es Bangla utilizes trusted third-party services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Used for secure user login and Google OAuth identity verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Used for cloud database synchronization of student test scores, study streaks, and entitlement records.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver critical study announcements, updates, and exam alerts.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app integrity, core libraries, and performance monitoring.

Each third-party service provider operates under its own strict privacy policy conforming to international security benchmarks. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

🛡️ 5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect student data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the app and cloud servers is encrypted using modern TLS / HTTPS protocols.
  • Secure Screen & PDF Protection: Educational PDF materials are rendered using protected viewers with screenshot-prevention safeguards to preserve academic integrity.
  • Role-Based Cloud Access: Database records can only be queried through verified authentication tokens linked to the student's unique UID.

🎓 6. Student & Youth Privacy Safety

Es Bangla is designed for students enrolled in Class 12 Higher Secondary education (typically aged 16 to 19). We do not collect unnecessary sensitive information, biometric data, or precise GPS location.

If you are under 18 years old, we recommend reviewing this policy with your parent or guardian before creating an account or purchasing any digital learning material.

🗑️ 7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can review your profile information, mock test analytics, and subscription status at any time directly in the Profile section of the app.
  • Right to Data Deletion: You can request permanent deletion of your account, mock test records, and email identifiers at any time.
How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
To request deletion, simply send an email to support@esbangla.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]". Your profile, test results, and server-side data will be permanently purged within 7 business days.
