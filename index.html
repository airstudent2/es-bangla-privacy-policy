Privacy Policy for Es Bangla

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, safety, and security of Higher Secondary (Class 12) students using the Es Bangla learning platform.

📅 Last Updated: October 30, 2026 • ✅ Effective Date: October 30, 2026
1. Overview 2. Information We Collect 3. Use of Information 4. Third-Party Partners 5. Data Protection 6. Student Privacy Safety 7. Data Deletion Rights 8. Contact Us

📖 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Es Bangla ("we", "our", or "us"). Es Bangla is a dedicated educational mobile application designed for Higher Secondary students preparing for Semester 3 and Semester 4 board examinations.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects information when you browse our study syllabus, attempt mock tests, watch video lectures, and read digital notes.

Our Student-First Commitment: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. All information collected is strictly used to deliver curriculum content, maintain your learning progress, and support your exam preparation.

📱 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Es Bangla, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect basic profile details such as your name and email address to maintain your personal learning account.
  • Academic Selection: Your selected semester (Semester 3 or Semester 4) and preferred subjects to personalize your course recommendations.
  • Subscription Status: Information regarding active courses or test series unlocked on your account.

B. Learning & Usage Data

  • Study & Test Progress: Mock test scores, attempted question sets, and revision performance indicators to help you track your academic improvement.
  • Study Reminders: If you choose to schedule daily study reminders, your preferred alert times are saved on your device.
  • App Diagnostics: Standard technical information such as device model, operating system version, and general diagnostic reports to ensure smooth application performance.
Guest Exploration: Students are welcome to explore our study guides and attempt free mock tests without creating an account. In guest mode, you can access free learning materials without providing personal contact information.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to facilitate an effective, personalized learning experience:

  • To provide access to Class 12 video lectures, chapter notes, and mock tests.
  • To maintain and display your personalized test performance and progress summaries.
  • To ensure that your activated courses and learning materials remain securely accessible on your account across app sessions.
  • To deliver helpful study reminders and important academic announcements (which can be turned off anytime in device settings).
  • To protect the platform against misuse, abuse, and unauthorized content redistribution.

☁️ 4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Services

To deliver a reliable, high-speed, and secure educational service, we collaborate with industry-leading technology partners:

  • Sign-In & Identity Verification: Trusted authentication services (such as Google Sign-In) to provide secure and effortless account access.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Reputable, global cloud hosting partners to reliably store academic content, sync test results, and deliver study updates.
  • Alert & Messaging Services: Standard messaging infrastructure to deliver optional study reminders and syllabus updates.

All third-party infrastructure providers operate under strict confidentiality obligations and adhere to rigorous international data protection standards.

🛡️ 5. Data Security & Protection

We take the security of student data very seriously and implement multi-layered safeguards:

  • Encrypted Communications: All data transmitted between the mobile application and cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.
  • Access Safeguards: Access to user profile data and learning history is strictly confined to authenticated account holders.
  • Content Security: Digital study materials and notes are protected by technical controls to preserve academic integrity.
  • Regular Maintenance: We regularly review and upgrade our security practices to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

🎓 6. Student & Youth Privacy Safety

Es Bangla is tailored for students preparing for Class 12 Higher Secondary examinations (typically ages 16 and above). We do not collect sensitive personal data, financial credentials, or location tracking.

We encourage younger students to review these policies with a parent, guardian, or teacher before creating an account.

🗑️ 7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your personal information:

  • Access & Review: You can view your account profile, learning history, and test analytics anytime within the application.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated test records at any time.
How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
To permanently remove your account and associated learning records, send an email to support@esbangla.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]". Your profile and records will be securely removed within 7 business days.
